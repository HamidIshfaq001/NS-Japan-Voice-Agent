# Documentation

## Documents you receive as the importer
1. Bill of Lading (B/L)
2. Insurance Document (only if your purchase is CIF)
3. Export Certificate (also called the "Deregistration Certificate" or
   "Cancellation of Registration" form)
4. Commercial Invoice

If your government requires them, we also send:
5. Inspection Certificate
6. Export Permit (also called the "Bill of Entry")

## Verifying the B/L before dispatch
Yes - we upload a PDF of your B/L and other documents to your CAP link so you can verify
that all information is correct before we send the originals.

## Altering documents
We will not change vehicle information on the B/L or on any other document. Altering
these documents is illegal and NS Japan Auto Ltd. does not participate in illegal
actions, regardless of the reason. The same applies to changing a vehicle's odometer
reading - that is illegal and we will not do it.

## Packing list
We can provide a packing list, and we can also send the Commercial Invoice. All relevant
information found on the packing list is included on the Commercial Invoice.

## Tracking your documents
Yes. Once your documents are processed for dispatch you can track them with the DHL
online tracking number. The tracking number on your CAP link turns into a DHL tracking
log when you click it.

If the DHL tracking log is not yet showing movement, wait 24 business hours before
reporting a problem. The log only starts once the package has been physically picked up
and scanned into DHL's system. Because our staff processes dispatches all day, your
dispatch may be processed after the last scheduled DHL pickup and will be collected on
the next business day.

## Buying on behalf of a client
If you are buying for a client, you may use the client's name and address as the DHL
delivery address, provided this causes no problem with the destination country's rules
and regulations for importing vehicles.

## Two sets of the B/L
Not possible. Shipping companies issue only one set of the B/L per cargo, regardless of
requests for additional copies.

## Clearing customs with a PDF B/L
No. You can complete about 60 percent of the customs clearing procedure using PDFs of
the B/L, but the rest requires the original B/L to be turned in.
