# Glossary of Terms

- Bill of Entry - see Export Permit.
- B/L (Bill of Lading) - a document issued by a carrier or its agent to the shipper
  as a contract of carriage of goods. It is also a receipt for cargo accepted for
  transportation, and it must be presented to take delivery at the destination.
- C&F - Cost and Freight. The price quoted does not include insurance but includes
  all expenses up to a named port of discharge.
- Cancellation of Registration Form - see Export Certificate.
- CAP - Car-Arrival-Progress. The buyer's personal web page on nsjapanautos.com for
  tracking the shipment process. Provided once payment is acknowledged, usually within a week.
- CIF - Cost, Insurance and Freight. The price quoted includes insurance and all
  other charges up to the named port of discharge.
- Consignee - the vehicle's owner-to-be, as named on the B/L.
- DHL Delivery Address - the delivery address for the shipping documents (not the
  vehicle), used to dispatch documents to the buyer.
- EAA - the East African authority body referenced for Tanzania and Uganda imports.
- ETA - Estimated Time of Arrival / Departure; when a ship journey is expected at or
  from a named city or port.
- Export Certificate - confirms the noted items are being legally exported from their
  country of origin (i.e. the car's previous registration in Japan has been cancelled).
- Export Permit - issued by the Japanese Customs Office; ensures the shipment complies
  with applicable export control measures.
- FOB - 1) NS Japan Auto Ltd.'s price of the car itself. 2) Free On Board: the price
  includes all charges up to placing the goods on board a ship at the port of departure.
- Forwarding Agent - a person or company that arranges transportation of cargo from
  the port of discharge to the vehicle's destination country.
- Import Permit - a document issued by certain governments giving permission to
  import goods.
- Inspection Certificate - certifies the vehicle passed the required pre-export
  inspection and meets required specifications, in good condition and correct quantity
  when leaving the port of departure.
- Insurance Document - proof of insurance for CIF shipments.
- Intertek - Intertek International Ltd., appointed by the Government of Mozambique
  to carry out pre-shipment inspections on all imported vehicles.
- ITAC - International Trade Administration Commission of South Africa; manages
  administration of international trade including import and export control.
- JEVIC - Japan Export Vehicle Inspection Center Co., Ltd.; specialises in
  pre-shipment inspection and certification of cargo. Its inspection certificate is
  required by the Kenyan and Zambian governments.
- LOA - Letter of Authority; required by certain governments (e.g. South Africa via
  NRCS) for importing a vehicle.
- Notify Party - the person the shipping agent contacts to confirm the vessel's
  arrival date at the port.
- NRCS - National Regulator for Compulsory Specifications (South Africa).
- Packing List - an itemised list included in the shipment giving quantity,
  description and weight of the contents.
- Performa Invoice - the final estimate in the form of an invoice, based on the
  chosen vehicle and shipping conditions.
- Port of Departure - the departure point of a ship or shipment.
- Port of Discharge - where a vessel is off-loaded and shipments are dispersed to
  their respective consignees.
- Shipping Agent - an agency that handles shipments and cargo at ports and harbours
  on behalf of shipping companies.
- Transshipment - the transfer of a shipment from one vessel to another while in
  transit to its final destination.
- TT Copy - Telegraphic Transfer Copy; the paper copy of a bank transfer.
- Under Offer - an offer to purchase has been made, making the vehicle unavailable
  for other customers to make an offer or receive a quote.
- Vanning Charge / Fee - the fee for loading and securing a shipping container.
- Vehicle Destination - the country where the vehicle will be registered for use.
