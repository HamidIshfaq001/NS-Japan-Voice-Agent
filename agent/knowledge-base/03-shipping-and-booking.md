# Shipping, Booking and Tracking

## What is CAP
CAP stands for Car-Arrival-Progress. It is your personal web page on the nsjapanautos.com
website for viewing the progress of your shipment. Once our staff acknowledges your
payment (usually within a week), a link to your CAP is sent to you.

Your CAP link shows the shipping schedule, vessel progress, tracking details, the PDF of
your inspection certificate, and PDFs of your B/L and other documents.

on past experience and are not guaranteed.

## Information required to book a shipping vessel
Most of this is required by the contracted shipping companies, not by NS Japan Auto Ltd.

- Port of discharge - your nearest port where the vessel will unload your cargo.
- Vehicle destination - where the vehicle will actually be used; the country where
  it will be registered.
- Consignee - the vehicle's "owner-to-be". Provide the consignee's full real name
  exactly as shown on their passport or ID, full address without abbreviations, and
  phone number(s). All of this is mandatory to receive your B/L from the shipping company.
- Notify party - the person the shipping agent contacts to confirm the vessel's
  arrival date at the port. If you have a private clearing agent you may list them here,
  though it is not mandatory. If you have not hired an agent, simply write
  "Same as Consignee".
- DHL delivery address - where we mail your documents, including your B/L, by DHL
  courier. Provide the recipient's full name, full address without abbreviations, and
  phone number(s).

Important warnings:
- Your booking will be delayed by the shipping company if the consignee's full name and
  address are not provided.
- The consignee's name cannot have any spelling mistakes. Even the smallest error can
  require a B/L amendment, which costs additional fees and time.
- Incomplete DHL information may delay the dispatch of your documents.

## Transshipment
If you see two shipping vessels listed on your CAP or in online tracking, this is
transshipment: your cargo is loaded onto one vessel departing Japan and reloaded onto
another before reaching its destination. This is decided by the contracted shipping
companies; as exporters we have no authority to intervene. Transshipped cargo usually
takes longer to reach its final destination.

## Schedule changes and delays
Changes and delays to the shipping schedule are made by the contracted shipping
companies. As exporters we do not have authority to intervene in their decisions.

## Blank ETA
If the vessel's ETA is blank on your CAP link, the contracted shipping company has not
yet decided it. The CAP link reflects the shipping company's decisions directly and
immediately. Please wait - it will populate once decided.

## Tracking
Tracking information for the shipping vessel is available and we can provide it. Use
your CAP link or the tracking number to check whether the vessel departed as scheduled
and whether your vehicle was loaded.

## Actual arrival date
Two ways to get it:
1. The shipping agent contacts the Notify Party listed on the B/L about the arrival.
2. You contact the shipping agent on your end about one week before the vessel's ETA.
   Find the carrier (shipping company) name on your CAP link or online tracking.

Contact your local clearing agent before your vessel's ETA.

## Pre-export inspection
Some countries require a mandatory pre-export inspection before the vehicle is exported.
Visit your country's government authorities to obtain the required pre-export inspection
list. Many countries that did not enforce this in the past are now imposing large fines
for violations. Make sure your purchase is inspection-inclusive if your government
requires it.

A PDF of the inspection certificate is uploaded to your CAP link once received from the
inspection agency.

## Auto parts inside a vehicle
We are not allowed to put anything inside the vehicle unless it is shipped by container.
