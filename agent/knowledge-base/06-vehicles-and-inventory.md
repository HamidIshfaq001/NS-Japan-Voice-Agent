# Vehicles and Inventory

## Inventory size
We maintain a constant inventory of over 12,000 vehicles across our network, with new
purchases every day. The live stock list published on nsjapanautos.com is a subset of
this and changes constantly.

## Requesting a vehicle not on the website
Absolutely possible. Tell us what you are looking for and our professional staff will do
our best to find the vehicle that best suits your request. This is one of our most
common requests - always offer it.

## Vehicle condition
We provide detailed pictures of each vehicle to help with your buying decision.

## Manufactured year
We list the manufactured year when the information is available. We cannot guarantee it
because it is provided by a third party, and NS Japan Auto Ltd. is not responsible for
any losses, damages or troubles caused by this information.

## Manuals
Vehicle manuals are in Japanese. Because we sell Japanese vehicles previously owned and
operated in Japan, any provided manual will be in Japanese, not English.

## Spare keys
It depends on the vehicle. If there are no spare keys in the glove compartment, they are
not provided, and you are responsible for making copies when you receive the vehicle.

## Accessories and installations
We can install additional items at extra cost. Tell us what you want installed before
asking for the Performa Invoice.

## Odometer readings
Changing a vehicle's odometer reading is illegal. NS Japan Auto Ltd. does not participate
in illegal actions, regardless of the reason.

## "Under offer"
A vehicle is "under offer" when a customer has made a formal offer to purchase it but we
have not yet confirmed their payment. If the offer is accepted and payment confirmed, it
becomes SOLD and is no longer available. If the offer is rejected or expires, the vehicle
returns to inventory and becomes available to other customers.

## Visiting in person
You cannot visit our storage yard - we do not allow anyone in, for security reasons.
You can visit our office by appointment; schedule in advance by email or phone.

## Buying for use inside Japan
Not possible. As exporters we do not sell vehicles for domestic use in Japan.

## Auto parts
We handle auto parts inquiries. For an auto parts request we need: vehicle name, model
code, your name, email, phone, ship-from location, address, city, state/province,
zip/postal code, country, and the auto part detail.

Note: we cannot ship auto parts inside a purchased vehicle unless it is shipped by container.
