# Buying and Payment

## How to buy a vehicle (the process)
1. Visit nsjapanautos.com and click the vehicle you like to open the Total Price Calculator.
2. Fill in all required information in the calculator and choose your destination in the
   "Nearest Port / City" drop-down menu.
3. Click "CALCULATE" to get a free price quote.
4. Fill in your personal information in the "Free Quote" section and click "FREE QUOTE"
   to send us an inquiry.
5. Our staff responds and the negotiation process begins.
6. Once you and NS Japan Auto Ltd. agree on pricing and conditions, you are notified.
7. You receive the Performa Invoice (the final estimate in invoice form).
8. You pay by bank remittance or credit card.
9. Once payment is acknowledged (usually within a week), we send you your CAP link to
   track the shipment.

The calculated price is the amount due from the buyer and must be paid as a one-time
payment. Installments are not accepted.

## What is a Performa Invoice
The "final estimate" in the form of an invoice, based on the chosen vehicle and the
agreed shipping conditions.

## What the FOB price includes
The FOB price shown on nsjapanautos.com is the price of the vehicle only.

It does NOT include: freight charges, storage charges, taxes, or duties.
Freight charges are added to the FOB price when you purchase.

After the vehicle arrives you are responsible for port clearing costs, import duties,
taxes and any other fees required by your country and/or the port of discharge.
Contact your local authorities or a reputable clearing agent for details. Our trained
agents are also available to assist you as clearing agents.

## Price terms
- FOB — Free On Board. The vehicle price; all charges up to placing the goods on board
  the ship at the port of departure.
- C&F — Cost and Freight. Excludes insurance, includes all expenses up to the named
  port of discharge.
- CIF — Cost, Insurance and Freight. Includes insurance and all other charges up to the
  named port of discharge.

## Currencies accepted
Primarily U.S. dollars. Japanese yen is accepted under certain circumstances.
No other currencies are accepted.

## Payment methods
Bank wire transfer (bank remittance) and credit card.

Cash is never accepted under any circumstances. All payments must be by bank remittance
or credit card so both buyer and seller have an official written record of the transaction.

## Payment timing
- Bank remittance: allow a minimum of three (3) business days for the transfer to be
  reflected in our bank account. This is the average time for interbank transactions.
- Credit card: at least 1 day, to match your information such as name and address.

Using someone else's card is strictly prohibited under any circumstances; such a case
can be referred to the crime control authority.

## Bank fees
The buyer is responsible for all bank fees. Confirm the cost of this fee with your bank
before closing the purchase agreement with our staff.

## TT Copy
Once you submit your payment for transfer at your bank you receive a Telegraphic
Transfer Copy (TT Copy) as proof of remittance. A deposit slip may replace the TT Copy
only if your bank does not issue the TT Copy immediately.

## Receipts
We can provide a receipt on demand. The bank remittance itself also provides an official
written record of the transaction for both buyer and seller.

## Discount codes
Enter your discount code in the discount code field in the vehicle page's "Free Quote"
section. Using a discount code means you agree to all applicable terms and conditions
provided by NS Japan Auto Ltd.

## City delivery (home delivery)
A service for transferring the vehicle from the port of discharge to a designated
city or border for delivery to the consignee.

City delivery is NOT guaranteed — it must be confirmed with a staff member, and it must
be quoted separately because the price differs significantly from a port-only price.

## Email address policy
To keep the vehicle buying process as secure as possible we accept only one email
address per customer. Do not use a work and a personal address for the same purchase.
