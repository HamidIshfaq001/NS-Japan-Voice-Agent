# Country Regulations

General rule: for export and import rules and procedures for your country, including
pre-export inspection lists, contact your country's appropriate government authorities.
Before importing, research what is expected of you as a vehicle importer in your country
and understand all risks and responsibilities.

## Tanzania and Uganda
Refer to EAA and each country's respective Revenue Authority (Tanzania Revenue Authority
and Uganda Revenue Authority).

## Kenya
Refer to JEVIC, the Kenya Bureau of Standards, and the Kenya Revenue Authority.

## Zambia
Refer to JEVIC and the Zambia Revenue Authority.

## Mozambique
Refer to Intertek and the Mozambique Revenue Authority.

## South Africa
Refer to the International Trade Administration Commission (ITAC) and the National
Regulator for Compulsory Specifications (NRCS).

The government of South Africa restricts the import of foreign cars to those that have
received an Import Permit from ITAC and a Letter of Authority (LOA) from the NRCS.

## Caribbean countries and Russia
Shipments to these areas sometimes require the consignee's postal code on the B/L. This
is required when the vessel travels through Caribbean areas controlled by European Union
countries, and violating it may result in a penalty charge to the consignee at the port
of discharge.

Even if you have successfully imported a vehicle without providing a postal code in the
past, that does not guarantee it will not be needed for future shipments - each vessel's
sailing route is different.
